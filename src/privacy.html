Zero Data Collection

Huli Meke is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This game was built as a free, offline-first traditional board game experience.

1. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, track, transmit, or share any personal information, identifiers, locations, or usage data whatsoever.

2. Local Storage

Huli Meke stores minimal preference data strictly inside your browser's private local storage:

  • Sound effects preference (On / Off)
  • In-game hints preference (On / Off)
  • Aggregate win/loss counters for games played on your device

This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to any server or third party, and can be cleared at any time via the "Reset stats" button in the Settings screen.

3. Third Parties & Tracking

There are zero analytics trackers, zero advertising networks, zero user accounts, and zero third-party scripts embedded in this application.

4. Offline Operation

Once installed or loaded in your browser, Huli Meke operates entirely offline through standard browser service worker caching.

5. Open Source

The full source code for Huli Meke is open source and hosted publicly on GitHub.
